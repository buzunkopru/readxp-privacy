# ReadXP — Copyright and Takedown Requests

**Last Updated:** October 28, 2026  
**Contact:** readxp.app@gmail.com

---

ReadXP shows book information — titles, authors, page counts and cover images — from public book catalogues, including Google Books, Apple Books, Open Library and Hardcover, and from facts that readers add. Cover images come from those catalogues; ReadXP does not claim any right in them.

If you believe a cover image or other material shown in ReadXP infringes your copyright, write to **readxp.app@gmail.com** with:

1. your name and how to reach you;
2. the work you believe is infringed;
3. the book in ReadXP where it appears (its title and author, and a screenshot if you can);
4. a statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law;
5. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the owner of the right, or authorised to act for the owner;
6. your physical or electronic signature.

We remove or hide the material promptly once we receive a complete notice, and we let the person who added it know where possible.
